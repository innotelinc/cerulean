Cerulean owns the Trust layer of the Innotel Platform Stack: certificate lifecycle and ACME automation, wildcard Let's Encrypt into BIND via TSIG-signed dynamic updates, an internal CA for client certificates, DNS health and compliance reporting — with Authentik SSO and Infisical secrets.

Trust, automated end to end.

Every certificate, zone and client cert issued, rotated and reported — no SSH sessions, no spreadsheets.

🛡️

Certificate lifecycle

Let's Encrypt issuance — regular and wildcard via DNS-01 — with automatic renewal and deployment.

🌐

DNS automation

Authoritative BIND management through TSIG (RFC 2136): add a domain, get its zone wired.

🔑

Internal PKI

A private CA for client certificates — issue, revoke and monitor device certs from the dashboard.

📋

Compliance reporting

Trust scoring, DNS health checks and compliance evidence for audits.

🔐

Authentik SSO

Every surface behind one identity provider at auth.cerulean.innotel.us.

🤫

Infisical secrets

TSIG keys and CA keys live in Infisical (SecretOps) — resolved at runtime, never in git.

The Trust layer of the stack.

Cerulean consumes Authentik (identity) and Infisical (secrets) and provides trust services to every other platform.

📦

Cerulean server

API + dashboard — certificate, DNS and PKI control plane.

🌐

BIND

Authoritative DNS, updated via TSIG-signed dynamic updates.

🛡️

ACME client

Let's Encrypt issuance, wildcard via DNS-01, auto-renewal.

🔑

Internal CA

Client-certificate issuance for device trust across the ecosystem.

$ ./scripts/setup.sh
>> [bind] generated TSIG key, configured zones
>> [acme] wildcard *.innotel.us issued (DNS-01)
>> [infisical] CA + TSIG secrets mirrored to Infisical
$ cerulean cert issue api.example.com
certificate issued · auto-renewal armed
